# Floral photo sources

The bouquets in this folder (`bouquet-a/b/c.webp`) are composed from real
photographs released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication: free for
commercial use, no attribution required). Backgrounds were removed and the
cut-outs arranged into corner bouquets for the wedding designs (start screen,
print frame and GIF frame).
